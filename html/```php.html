Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

1. What is Scholarship Finder?

Scholarship Finder helps students find scholarship opportunities.

2. How can I find scholarships?

Visit the Scholarships page to view available scholarship information.

3. Can every student apply for every scholarship?

No. Each scholarship may have different requirements. Read the requirements before applying.

4. Does Scholarship Finder give out scholarships?

No. The website helps students find opportunities. Scholarship providers decide who receives awards.

5. How can I contact the team?

Visit the Contact page and send us a message.
